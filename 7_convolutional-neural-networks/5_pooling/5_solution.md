- Maximum pooling takes into account just the maximum entry within the window, and no matter how we change the non-maximum entries (except making them the new maximum), the output doesn't change. On the other hand, average pooling takes into account each pixel of the image for each window position. This means that change in every pixel no matter it is maximum or not changes the output in average pooling case. 
In images, identification of features is generally of interest. And the features usually correspond to maximum intensities and not average intensities over an area. Hence, that I believe causes average pooling to have usually a high variance than maximum pooling. 
Also the fact that max pooling introduces non-linearity into the system allows the model to be more flexible in the patterns it can recognize.
