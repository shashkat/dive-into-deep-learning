- The statistical benefits of a stride larger than 1 would be that in the output, the individual entries with increasing stride are less dependent on each other (correlated). This means we get more independent pieces of information from the original image with increasing stride. There can be multiple scenarios in which we prefer to have the pixels of the output image as uncorrelated as possible. One example of this that comes to my mind is that suppose we were to flatten the output and pass it through a MLP and get probabilities for different classes into which we had to classify the original image. In such a case it would be preferred that the input to the MLP has as less correlated terms as possible, as that leads to more robust and generalized model.
